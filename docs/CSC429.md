## Corrected hybrid design

The previous attempt was rejected because it invented descriptive questions.
The corrected design is:

- Build a question bank from the **real past-paper questions**, preserving each
  question's original figure, table or image asset.
- For each seed, **jumble** questions drawn from different real papers into the
  planned Part A/Part B structure.
- Use the generator to produce **controlled randomized variants** of the
  selected questions where parameterization is appropriate; do not replace
  real descriptive/diagram questions with invented prose.
- Presentation is per asset type: a figure is captured from the original page;
  a table is recreated as a native Word table; a figure region that is mostly
  text is recreated as native text. Captions are metadata only and are never
  captured into an asset or printed as a separate question block.
- Every lettered/roman sub-part uses native Word list numbering, so each
  ``a)``/``b)`` and ``i.``/``ii.`` item starts with a list paragraph mark and
  the numbering restarts per question.
- Keep the source paper, page/asset reference and marking evidence for every
  bank item.

The rejected trial is archived at
`outputs/archive/csc429_attempt1_20261105/` for audit. It is not used by any
pipeline path.

### Bank and generation architecture

- The 20 real papers contain **215 questions**: 19 papers have 10 Part A
  questions plus 1 Part B question; `CSC429-2013-12` has 5 Part A plus 1
  Part B. Six papers have a text layer; 14 are OCR-only.
- The rejected log had 213 entries: two whole questions were missing, marks
  were unreliable, and flattened text could not locate figures or tables.
  The new bank therefore uses the layout layer, not the flat corpus.
- Add `data/tracking/csc429/question_bank.json`. Each item records
  `bank_id`, `paper_id`, `source_pdf`, `source_page`, `source_kind`,
  `raw_text`, `reviewed_text`, `text_sha256`, `marks`, `topic`, `task`,
  `subparts`, `assets`, `marking`, `variant_policy` and `review_flags`.
- Asset records use relative paths under `data/tracking/csc429/assets/` and
  include `kind`, `path`, `source_page`, `bbox_pdf`, `page_image`, `dpi`,
  `sha256`, `caption` and `width_cm`. Figure crops are content-only and stop
  above the caption. Full-page images are provenance only. Tables are rebuilt
  from the layout layer; mixed figure/text regions are rebuilt as native text.
- The planner samples real bank items without replacement, jumbles them across
  source papers, enforces source diversity and the observed 10+1 mark
  structure, and records `bank_id`, `asset_ids` and `variant_params` per slot.
- Only parameterizable item types (data representation, cache addressing,
  assembly) may receive controlled numeric variants. Descriptive and diagram
  questions stay verbatim with their original asset. Computed answers are
  independently verified.
- Every bank item carries an explicit `marking` record. No official CSC429
  scheme was supplied. The owner authorised unofficial AI model answers on
  2026-11-06; they are cached by question id, labelled as unofficial, and
  carry a confidence level and assumptions rather than being presented as an
  official marking scheme.
- CSC402/CSC413 are deterministic template banks, not real-question
  recyclers, so CSC429 cannot copy their content pipeline. Their planner,
  validator and builder patterns are reusable.
