## Formatting

- **Every question has its own page.** The paper is six pages: cover and
  instructions, then Q1–Q5.
- **Q4 relational schema** is set in Consolas 10 pt, one caption per line, with
  6 pt between captions and 8 pt above/below the block, so it reads as schema
  rather than question prose.
- **Q5 business-rule dashes** have 10 pt of space before the first rule and
  after the last rule. Bullet glyphs in the dash list are normalised to `-`.
